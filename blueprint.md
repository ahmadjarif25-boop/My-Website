# **App Name**: BD News Hub

## Core Features:

- News Aggregation: Aggregate news articles from various Bangladeshi online newspapers.
- Categorized News: Display news in categories such as politics, sports, business, and technology.
- AI Summaries: Use AI to summarize lengthy news articles, providing key points.
- Search Functionality: Enable users to search for news articles based on keywords or topics.
- Personalized News Feed: Allow users to create a personalized news feed based on their interests. This can be accomplished via tool, which selects what kind of news articles to show.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Strong blue (#29ABE2) to convey trust and reliability.
- Background color: Light gray (#F0F2F5) for a clean and readable interface.
- Accent color: Yellow-orange (#F9A825) to highlight important news or categories.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans', a modern, humanist sans-serif font, is to be used for a balance of readability and a touch of personality.
- Simple, outline-style icons for categories and navigation, ensuring clarity and a modern look.
- Clean, grid-based layout with a focus on readability and easy navigation.
- Subtle animations for transitions and loading states, providing a smooth user experience.
